Growing eCommerce businesses rarely operate from a single platform. Orders may come in through multiple sales channels, while other systems support inventory, fulfillment, warehouse activity, production, customer service, or internal business processes. eCommerce system integration helps those platforms exchange the information the business needs so they can work together more effectively.
