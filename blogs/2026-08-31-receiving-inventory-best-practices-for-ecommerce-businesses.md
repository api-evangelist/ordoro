Inventory does not become useful the moment it arrives at the door. A supplier shipment may be delivered, but the work is not finished until the team checks what arrived, confirms it against the purchase order, updates inventory, handles any discrepancies, and makes the products available for orders. That is where receiving inventory comes in.
