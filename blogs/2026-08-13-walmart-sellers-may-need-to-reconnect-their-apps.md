Walmart Marketplace sellers who rely on third-party apps should pay attention to the latest Walmart app connection changes. An upcoming deadline could affect apps used to manage orders, inventory, shipping, and other parts of their business. Walmart is retiring an older method that approved Solution Providers use to connect to seller accounts.
