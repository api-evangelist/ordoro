Customer trust means everything to us, which makes this award especially meaningful. Ordoro has once again been recognized by SourceForge, earning the Summer 2026 Top Performer Award. The award is given to products with enough recent, highly rated user reviews to rank among the top 10% of favorably reviewed products on SourceForge.
