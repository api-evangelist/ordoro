When John Loftis walks onto The Boardsmith’s shop floor each morning, he sees more than a woodworking operation. He sees an eCommerce manufacturing workflow in motion, with a team moving between cutting, planing, sanding, cleaning, and finishing. Six or eight stages of butcher block production may be underway at once.
